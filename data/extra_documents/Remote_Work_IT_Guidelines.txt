## 1. Approved equipment
Use only your company-managed laptop for work. Personal laptops and tablets must not be used to open company files or sign in to company systems.

## 2. Home network
- Change the default administrator password on your home router.
- Use WPA2 or WPA3 encryption on your home Wi-Fi.
- Keep the router firmware up to date.

## 3. Public places
When working in a cafe, hotel or airport:
- Connect to the VPN before opening any internal system.
- Do not use public USB charging ports. Use your own power adapter.
- Use a privacy screen or position yourself so others cannot read your screen.
- Lock your laptop with Windows key + L whenever you step away.

## 4. Video calls from home
Use Microsoft Teams for all work meetings. Blur your background if your home is visible. Do not record a meeting unless every participant has been told.

## 5. Working abroad
Working from another country for more than 5 working days needs manager approval and a note to the IT Service Desk so that sign-in alerts from the new location are not mistaken for an attack.

## 6. Internet problems at home
1. Restart your router and wait 3 minutes.
2. Try a wired connection if you have one.
3. If you can browse but cannot reach internal sites, reconnect the VPN.
4. If the VPN will not connect, follow the VPN Connection Guide.

## 7. Printing at home
Printing company documents at home is not permitted. Use the office printers or share the document electronically.
