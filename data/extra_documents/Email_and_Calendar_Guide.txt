## 1. Mailbox size
Every Acme Demo Corp employee has a 50 GB Outlook mailbox. You receive a warning at 45 GB. At 50 GB you can no longer send mail until you free space. Delete large attachments or use Online Archive to move old mail.

## 2. Online Archive
Online Archive is turned on automatically. Mail older than 2 years moves to the archive on its own. Open the archive from the left folder pane in Outlook, under "Online Archive".

## 3. Setting an out-of-office reply
1. In Outlook, choose File, then Automatic Replies.
2. Select "Send automatic replies" and set the start and end dates.
3. Write one message for colleagues and, if needed, a separate one for people outside the company.
4. Click OK. The reply stops by itself on the end date.

## 4. Shared mailboxes
Team mailboxes such as a department inbox are shared mailboxes. To get access, open a ticket in the Service Portal and choose "Email access". The mailbox owner must approve. Access normally appears in Outlook within 60 minutes. Mail sent from a shared mailbox shows the shared address as the sender.

## 5. Attachment limits
The maximum email size is 25 MB including attachments. For larger files, upload to OneDrive and send a link instead. Executable files (.exe, .bat, .js) are blocked in attachments.

## 6. Reporting suspicious email
Use the "Report Phish" button on the Outlook ribbon. Do not click links or open attachments in the message. Reporting sends the message to the security team without forwarding it.

## 7. Calendar tips
- Meeting rooms are booked by adding the room as a resource in the meeting invite.
- Rooms are released automatically if nobody accepts the meeting check-in within 15 minutes.
- To share your calendar, choose Share on the Calendar tab and pick the colleague. Colleagues see free/busy by default.
