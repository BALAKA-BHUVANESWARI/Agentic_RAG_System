## 1. Before your first day
Your manager submits a "New Starter" request in the Service Portal at least 5 business days before your start date. IT then prepares your laptop, user account and email address.

## 2. Collecting your laptop
Collect your laptop from the IT Service Desk on your first morning between 9:00 and 10:00. Bring photo identification. You will sign for the laptop and receive a power adapter and a laptop bag.

## 3. First sign-in
1. Start the laptop and connect to Acme-Corp Wi-Fi or a wired dock.
2. Sign in with the temporary password on your welcome letter.
3. You must choose a new password immediately.
4. Enrol in multi-factor authentication when prompted. See the MFA Guidelines for the steps.

## 4. Accounts you receive on day one
- Company email and calendar (Outlook)
- Microsoft Teams
- OneDrive with 1 TB of storage
- Access to the intranet
- Access to Software Center for approved applications

## 5. Access that needs a request
VPN access, shared mailboxes and specialist software are not given automatically. Ask your manager to request them in the Service Portal.

## 6. First-week checklist
- Complete the security awareness training in the learning portal within 7 days.
- Install the Acme SecureLink VPN client from Software Center.
- Set up your voicemail and Teams profile picture.
- Add your phone number to your account recovery details.

## 7. Getting help
Contact the IT Service Desk through the Service Portal or at extension 5000 on weekdays between 8:00 and 18:00.
