## 1. Data classification
Company data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted. Handle data according to the highest classification it contains.

## 2. Devices
- Lock your screen whenever you leave your laptop. Screens lock automatically after 10 minutes.
- USB storage devices are blocked on company laptops.
- Personal cloud storage services must not be used for company data.

## 3. Lost or stolen devices
Report a lost or stolen laptop or phone to the IT Service Desk within 1 hour of noticing. IT will lock and wipe the device remotely.

## 4. Phishing
Do not click links or open attachments in unexpected messages. Use the Report Phishing button in Outlook to report suspicious email. If you clicked a link or entered your password, contact the IT Service Desk immediately.

## 5. Working in public
Do not work on Confidential data in public places where others can see your screen. Use a privacy screen where available.

## 6. Incident reporting
All suspected security incidents must be reported to IT Security as soon as possible, no later than 24 hours after discovery.
