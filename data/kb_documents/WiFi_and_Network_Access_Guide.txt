## 1. Networks in the office
- Acme-Corp: for company laptops. Uses certificate-based sign-in; managed laptops join automatically.
- Acme-Guest: for visitors. Reception issues a daily access code that expires at midnight.

## 2. Connecting a company laptop
1. Select Acme-Corp from the Wi-Fi list.
2. If asked, sign in with your company username and password.
3. Accept the company certificate prompt.

## 3. Personal devices
Personal phones may join Acme-Guest only. Personal laptops must not be connected to Acme-Corp.

## 4. Troubleshooting
- Forget the network and join again.
- Restart the laptop.
- If the certificate has expired, open a ticket with the IT Service Desk. Certificates renew automatically every 12 months.

## 5. Wired network
Docking stations in meeting rooms provide wired access. Plug in the cable and wait about 30 seconds.
