## 1. Before you contact IT
Save your work, then restart the laptop. A full restart (not sleep) fixes many problems. Restart at least once a week.

## 2. Laptop is slow
1. Close applications you are not using.
2. Open Task Manager (Ctrl+Shift+Esc) and check which program uses the most memory or CPU.
3. Make sure at least 10 percent of disk space is free.
4. Install pending updates from Acme Software Center and restart.

## 3. Laptop will not turn on
1. Connect the charger and wait 15 minutes.
2. Hold the power button for 15 seconds, release, then press it once.
3. If it still does not start, contact the IT Service Desk. A loaner laptop is normally available within 1 business day.

## 4. Wi-Fi keeps disconnecting
Turn Wi-Fi off and on, forget and rejoin the network, then restart. See the Wi-Fi and Network Access Guide for details.

## 5. Blue screen or repeated crashes
Note the stop code shown on screen, restart, and open a ticket with that code. Do not reinstall Windows yourself.

## 6. Microsoft Teams problems on your laptop
If Teams is slow or will not start, quit it from the system tray, clear the Teams cache using the "Reset Teams" shortcut in Acme Software Center, and sign in again.

## 7. Battery
Replace the battery if it lasts under 2 hours on a full charge. Request a replacement through the Service Desk portal.
