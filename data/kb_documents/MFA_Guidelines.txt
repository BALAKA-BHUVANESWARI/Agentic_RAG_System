## 1. Requirement
MFA is mandatory for all employees on email, VPN, the intranet and any cloud application using company single sign-on.

## 2. Enrolling
1. Install the Acme Authenticator app on your phone from the official app store.
2. Go to mfa.acme-demo.example and sign in with your company credentials.
3. Choose "Add device" and scan the QR code with the app.
4. Enter the six-digit code to finish enrolment.
5. Download your 10 one-time backup codes and store them in Acme Vault.

## 3. Approving sign-in prompts
Sign-ins use number matching: enter the number shown on the sign-in screen into the app. Only approve prompts that you started yourself.

## 4. Unexpected prompts
If you receive an MFA prompt you did not start, tap Deny and report it to IT Security through the Report Phishing button or the Service Desk within 1 hour. Then change your password.

## 5. Lost or replaced phone
Contact the IT Service Desk. Your identity will be verified before MFA is reset. You can use a backup code to sign in while you wait. Enrol the new phone as soon as access is restored.

## 6. Hardware security keys
Administrators and privileged accounts must use a hardware security key in addition to the authenticator app.
