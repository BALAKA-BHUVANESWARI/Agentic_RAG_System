## 1. Purpose
This policy defines password requirements for all Acme Demo Corp accounts.

## 2. Requirements
- Minimum length: 14 characters.
- Use a passphrase or a random string; complexity rules (symbols, digits) are optional if the length requirement is met.
- Passwords must not contain your name, username or the company name.
- You may not reuse any of your last 12 passwords.

## 3. Expiry
Passwords do not expire on a fixed schedule. A password must be changed within 24 hours if you suspect it has been compromised, or if IT Security requires a reset.

## 4. Account lockout
After 5 failed sign-in attempts the account is locked for 15 minutes.

## 5. Password manager
Employees should store passwords in Acme Vault, the approved password manager. Do not store passwords in spreadsheets, notes apps or browsers.

## 6. Resetting a forgotten password
Use the self-service portal at pwreset.acme-demo.example. You will need to verify with MFA. If you cannot use MFA, contact the IT Service Desk.

## 7. Sharing
Never share your password with anyone, including IT staff. The Service Desk will never ask for your password.
