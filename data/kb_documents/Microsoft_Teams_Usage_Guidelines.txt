## 1. Purpose
Microsoft Teams is the approved tool for internal chat, meetings and file collaboration at Acme Demo Corp.

## 2. Meetings
- Schedule meetings from the Teams or Outlook calendar so a join link is created automatically.
- Join with your company account. Anonymous join is allowed only for external guests invited by an employee.
- Meeting recordings are stored for 120 days and then deleted automatically. Tell participants when you start recording.

## 3. Channels and chat
- Create a team only for a standing group or project. Use the naming format DEPT-Project-Name.
- Do not post passwords, customer personal data or restricted documents in chat.

## 4. Guest access
External guests may be added only after your manager approves the request through a ticket. Guest access expires after 90 days unless renewed.

## 5. File sharing
Share files from SharePoint or OneDrive links rather than attaching copies. Do not share files marked Confidential outside the company.

## 6. Status and notifications
Set your status to Do Not Disturb during focus time. Mute channels you do not need.

## 7. Getting help
For Teams problems on your own device, see the Laptop Troubleshooting Guide or open a ticket with the IT Service Desk.
