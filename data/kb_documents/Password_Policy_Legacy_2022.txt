## 1. Purpose
This document describes the password rules for Acme Demo Corp user accounts.

## 2. Requirements
- Minimum length: 8 characters.
- Must include an uppercase letter, a lowercase letter, a digit and a symbol.
- You may not reuse any of your last 5 passwords.

## 3. Expiry
Passwords must be changed every 90 days.

## 4. Account lockout
After 3 failed sign-in attempts the account is locked for 30 minutes.

## 5. Resetting a forgotten password
Call the IT Service Desk, who will issue a temporary password after verifying your identity.
